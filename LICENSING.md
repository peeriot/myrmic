## License zones

| Where | License | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `swarm/` (except `swarm/signal-layer/`), `embedded/`, `tests/`, `benchmarks/` | `GPL-2.0-only` together with the Myrmic Exception | The Myrmic platform: runtime, self-organization, data layer, embedded ports, firmware, the host side of all interfaces, and the project's test and benchmark infrastructure |

Every crate declares its applicable license in `Cargo.toml`. File-level licensing is declared in [`REUSE.toml`](REUSE.toml) and, where present, in an `SPDX-License-Identifier` header. In the event of any inconsistency with this guide, the file-level license notice identifies the applicable licensing terms.

## What you can build

- **Applications (Cells) via the SDK:** your independently developed WASM modules are your code and may be licensed by you under terms of your choice, including proprietary. The SDK crates you compile in remain licensed under MIT or Apache 2.0. The Myrmic Exception permits such modules to be distributed together with the GPL-2.0-only covered platform without becoming subject to GPL-2.0-only solely because of that combination.
- **Signal-layer pipelines and modules:** your independently developed drivers and steps are your code and may be licensed by you under terms of your choice, including proprietary. On operating-system-based devices the generated pipeline program is permissive crates plus your code, running as a separate process. On microcontrollers your modules may be statically linked together with the GPL-2.0-only platform into a single firmware image. **Building and distributing that combined image is expressly permitted by the Myrmic Exception without requiring your modules to be licensed under GPL-2.0-only**, provided your code does not copy, modify or incorporate GPL-2.0-only covered code and interacts only through the unmodified official interfaces listed in [`EXCEPTION-SCOPE.md`](EXCEPTION-SCOPE.md).
- **Modifying the platform itself** (the GPL zone): GPL-2.0-only continues to apply. If you distribute your modifications, you must comply with the applicable terms of GPL-2.0-only, including distributing your modifications under the same license, or obtain a commercial license, where applicable (included in the EdgeVance subscription).

## The official interfaces

The Myrmic Exception applies to applications that interact with the platform **exclusively through the unmodified official interfaces** listed in [`EXCEPTION-SCOPE.md`](EXCEPTION-SCOPE.md) — the interface definitions named there, in the version stated there. What must stay unmodified is the interface itself: the host functions, the IPC protocol, the module contract. The SDK crates that implement those interfaces are libraries; you may use them as published, patch them or replace them with your own bindings, as long as what crosses the boundary is the unmodified official interface. That file is versioned with every release. Four of the listed libraries, `myrmic-sdk`, `myrmic-sdk-macros`, `myrmic-common` and `myrmic-signal-layer-types`, are published on crates.io; the remaining signal-layer libraries ship as part of the release itself. If you fork and extend the interfaces, Peeriot's additional permissions do not cover applications built against your extended interfaces.

## Code-generator output, config, third parties, trademarks

- **Output of the Myrmic code generators** — `myrmic-sdk-macros`, `pipeline-codegen`, `esp-codegen`, `linux-codegen` — produced from your own declarations (pipeline/module YAML, SDK macro annotations) is **not** subject to GPL-2.0-only copyleft merely because it was generated by one of those tools: it is yours, and you may put the output, including any Peeriot-authored boilerplate the generators emit into it, which is provided without conditions or attribution requirements, under terms of your choice. This holds however you use the output, including static linking into a firmware image. It concerns the *output* only — modifying a generator itself is governed by that generator's license. *(This paragraph is about deterministic code generation from your declarations — the compiler-style transformation these tools perform. It says nothing about AI/LLM-generated code, and nothing about the output of Myrmic's AI-based functions.)*
- Pipeline YAML, `descriptor.yaml`, board manifests and similar are **data** and do not become subject to GPL-2.0-only merely because they are processed or used by Myrmic.
- Third-party Rust dependencies remain under their own licenses. The dependency license policy the project enforces is [`deny.toml`](deny.toml); third-party code vendored into this repository is attributed in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE). `docker/` **orchestrates** third-party services under their own licenses; they are not part of Myrmic releases.

The Myrmic logo and other brand assets in this repository (for example `doc/images/myrmic-logo.svg`) are not covered by the licenses applicable to the software code, data or documentation.** Their use is subject to Peeriot's trademark policy, which will be published separately; until then, no rights in the brand assets are granted beyond those that apply by law.
