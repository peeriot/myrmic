# Community and Support
## Overview
Myrmic is built in the open, and community input helps shape its direction. This page points you to the right place for questions, feedback, and support.

## Contributing
Contributions of all sizes matter. You can help by improving docs, sharing demos, or contributing code.

- Documentation: fix gaps, clarify steps, add examples.
- Demos and examples: show real use cases.
- Codebase: bug fixes and improvements.

Start here: [Contributing](./14_contribution.md).

## Discussions
If you have a question about Myrmic, or want to help others, join the conversation:

- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/peeriot/myrmic/discussions)
- [Discord](https://discord.gg/zExh79pWgj)

For reproducible bugs with a minimal repro and logs, open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/peeriot/myrmic/issues).

## Social Media
Follow Myrmic on [X (@MyrmicOfficial)](https://x.com/MyrmicOfficial) and [YouTube (@MyrmicOfficial)](https://www.youtube.com/@MyrmicOfficial) for updates, demos, and videos.

## Code of Conduct
Be respectful and constructive. All contributors follow the project [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/peeriot/myrmic/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Security Reporting
Please report security issues privately, following the [Security policy](https://github.com/peeriot/myrmic/blob/master/SECURITY.md).

## Licensing questions

Whether your Cell, pipeline or firmware may stay proprietary, what to pass on when you ship Myrmic, and why contributors accept a CLA: the [Licensing](15_licensing.md) chapter answers the common cases, and [What You Can Build](15_licensing/01_what-you-can-build.md) is a step-by-step guide. If your case is not covered, ask in a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/peeriot/myrmic/discussions) - we would rather clarify than have you guess.
